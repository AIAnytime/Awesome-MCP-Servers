Set up in your client

Claude Code

Run this in a terminal:

claude mcp add --transport http growo-child-growth https://mcp.usegrowo.com/

Claude Desktop

Open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste this URL:

https://mcp.usegrowo.com/

Or bridge it through mcp-remote in claude_desktop_config.json (needs Node.js):

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "growo-child-growth": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": [
        "-y",
        "mcp-remote",
        "https://mcp.usegrowo.com/"
      ]
    }
  }
}

  • If the tools do not appear, open the client's MCP log: a 401 means the credentials were not accepted, and a timeout means the server did not answer.

birth_size_percentileread-onlyWhere a birth measurement sits on the Olsen 2010 intrauterine curves, for the number of completed weeks of …

Where a birth measurement sits on the Olsen 2010 intrauterine curves, for the number of completed weeks of pregnancy. This answers a different question from the growth charts: not how a child grows over time, but how big they were on the day they were born given how long the pregnancy ran. A 2.2 kg baby at 34 weeks and a 2.2 kg baby at 40 weeks are not the same situation, and the WHO charts cannot tell them apart. Returns the percentile, the z-score and the SGA/AGA/LGA category, which is a statistical classification and not a diagnosis.

gestationalWeeksintegerrequiredCompleted weeks of pregnancy at delivery, 23-41. Use the obstetric gestational age, not corrected age.
valuenumberrequiredThe measurement. Birth weight in GRAMS (3400, not 3.4) as the published tables and delivery rooms both quote it; length and head circumference in centimetres.
localestringoptionalLanguage for the disclaimer and any clinician note. Pass the language you will answer the user in: these are clinical safety texts written by a physician, and they should reach the reader as written rather than translated by the model. One of: en, tr

bmi_percentileread-onlyComputes BMI from weight and height, then places it on the BMI-for-age chart for the child's sex and age: the …

Computes BMI from weight and height, then places it on the BMI-for-age chart for the child's sex and age: the WHO standards under 24 months, the CDC 2000 growth charts from 24 months to 240 months (20 years), the split the CDC advises. From 24 months the result also carries the CDC percentile range it falls in (below the 5th, 5th to below the 85th, 85th to below the 95th, 95th and above). BMI in childhood is read against age and sex, never against the adult 18.5–25 bands. Same functions and tables as the child BMI calculator on usegrowo.com. Returns no interpretation.

corrected_ageread-onlyThe corrected age of a baby born before 37 weeks: the age since birth minus the weeks the baby was early. Use …

The corrected age of a baby born before 37 weeks: the age since birth minus the weeks the baby was early. Use it for 'what is my preemie's corrected / adjusted age', for reading milestones, and for choosing the age at which to plot growth. Takes the birth date and the gestational age at birth and returns the corrected age, the age since birth, the original due date, and the date by which correction usually stops (about 24 months since birth). Before the due date there is no corrected age, and the result says how many days remain. A baby born at 37 weeks or later needs no correction. Dates are calendar dates (YYYY-MM-DD).

growth_trendread-onlyWhat moved between two measurements of the same child: the percentile and z-score at each point, how far the …

What moved between two measurements of the same child: the percentile and z-score at each point, how far the position shifted, and how many of the drawn 3rd/15th/50th/85th/97th lines were crossed. Band crossings are the unit that matters — one is common while a child settles onto their own curve, two downward is the pattern clinicians review. NOT a velocity percentile: WHO's separate Growth Velocity Standards are not implemented here, so this reports movement across the attained-growth chart and never claims a percentile for the rate of gain itself.

target_heightread-onlyThe adult height a child is expected to reach, estimated from both parents' heights with Tanner's …

The adult height a child is expected to reach, estimated from both parents' heights with Tanner's mid-parental method: (mother + father +13 cm for a boy, -13 cm for a girl) / 2, with a range of 8.5 cm either side. Use it for 'how tall will my child be', 'mid-parental height' and 'child height predictor' questions. It is a statistical range, not a forecast for one child, and says nothing about the child's height today. Optionally, with the child's age (24-240 months) and current height, it adds a CDC projection: today's z-score on the CDC 2000 stature-for-age chart carried to age 20, with a range of one standard deviation either side; a height more than 5 SD from the CDC median for the age is refused as an entry error. Centimetres only: convert feet and inches first (5 ft 10 in = 177.8 cm).

ParameterTypeDescription
sexstringrequiredThe child's sex: the formula adds or subtracts 13 cm accordingly. One of: boy, girl
motherHeightCmnumberrequiredMother's height in centimetres.
fatherHeightCmnumberrequiredFather's height in centimetres.
childAgeMonthsnumberoptionalOptional, with childHeightCm: the child's age in months (2 to 20 years), for the CDC projection.
childHeightCmnumberoptionalOptional, with childAgeMonths: the child's current height in centimetres, measured standing.
localestringoptionalLanguage for the disclaimer and any clinician note. Pass the language you will answer the user in: these are clinical safety texts written by a physician, and they should reach the reader as written rather than translated by the model. One of: en, tr
